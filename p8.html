Vision & Mission

Vision

To be a centre of excellence in computer engineering education and research, producing globally competent and socially responsible professionals.

Mission
  • Provide quality education through innovative teaching-learning practices.
  • Promote research, innovation and industry collaboration.
  • Develop skilled engineers with strong ethical values.
  • Encourage lifelong learning and entrepreneurship.

Program Specific Outcomes

PSO 1

Analyze, design and develop efficient software solutions using modern programming languages and data structures.

PSO 2

Apply web, database, cloud and networking technologies to build scalable applications.

PSO 3

Work in teams using modern tools and pursue higher studies, research or entrepreneurship.
